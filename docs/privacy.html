Privacy

Updated 26 November 2026

Location

Nightwatch uses your watch's approximate location to calculate sun, moon, and tide times. This happens entirely on your watch. The last location is stored only on the watch.

Without location access

If you don't grant location access, Nightwatch estimates your location from your watch's time zone (its main city), so you still see sun, moon, and tide times. It does the same when your saved location doesn't match your current time zone.

Background location

Optional. If you choose "Allow all the time", the times follow you when you travel.

What we don't do

No data leaves your watch. Nightwatch has no network access, accounts, analytics, or ads. Nothing is shared or sold.

Removing data

Uninstalling the companion app deletes the stored location. You can turn off location access anytime in Settings > Apps > Nightwatch > Permissions.
