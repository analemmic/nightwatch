Privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

Nightwatch is a Wear OS watch face (com.analemmic.nightwatch.face) and its companion app, Nightwatch (com.analemmic.nightwatch.astro), published by Analemmic.

Location

The companion app uses your watch's approximate (coarse) location to calculate sunrise, sunset, twilight, moonrise and moonset times for the watch face, and to find the nearest tide gauge (from a table built into the app) for high and low tide times. The calculation runs entirely on your watch. The most recent approximate location is stored only on the watch, so the face keeps working without a fresh fix.

What we don't do

Nightwatch does not send your location or any other data off your watch. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no network access. Nothing is shared with or sold to third parties.

Background location

If you choose "Allow all the time", the app can update your location while the watch face is showing, so the times follow you when you travel. This is optional; without it, the app uses the location saved when you last opened it.

Removing data

Uninstalling the Nightwatch companion app deletes the stored location. You can revoke location access at any time in the watch's Settings > Apps > Nightwatch > Permissions.
