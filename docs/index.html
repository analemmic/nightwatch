Nightwatch

A 24-hour watch face for Wear OS that shows the whole day at once: daylight and twilight, the moon, and the tides, wherever you are.

Features

A 24-hour dial

Noon at the top, midnight at the bottom. The hour hand goes around once a day, so its angle tells you where you are between sunrise and sunset.

Daylight and twilight

Nested bands show day and civil, nautical and astronomical twilight for today, so you can see at a glance how much light is left. Sunrise and sunset are marked with lines and their times.

Solar noon and the seasons

A quiet line marks when the sun is highest. On an equinox or solstice, an amber line marks the exact moment.

The moon

An arc traces when the moon is up, from moonrise to moonset, with its current phase drawn at the moment it's highest in the sky.

Tides

Dots mark high and low water from the nearest of about 2,800 tide gauges worldwide (within 50 km).

Day and night palettes

Switches automatically at sunrise and sunset. The night palette is all deep red, easy on dark-adapted eyes. Or pick Day or Night in the face's settings.

Any day, past or future

Tap the centre to open a day scrubber: turn the crown to move through the days. Tap again for that day's table of every event, with tide heights.

Always on

Ambient mode keeps the same layout in a dimmer, low-power form, so the whole day stays readable at a glance.

Private by design. Everything is calculated on your watch. Your location never leaves it: Nightwatch has no network access, accounts, analytics or ads. Read the privacy policy.

Get it

Nightwatch is a watch face plus a small companion app that does the astronomy. It needs Wear OS 6 or later. Coming to Google Play; the source is on GitHub.

Sun, moon and tide times are calculated estimates. They are not suitable for navigation.
