Nightwatch

A 24-hour watch face for Wear OS: the sun, moon, and tides at a glance.

Features

24-hour dial

Noon at the top, midnight at the bottom.

Sun

Daylight and twilight shading, sunrise and sunset times, and solar noon.

Moon

An arc shows when the moon is up, with its phase.

Tides

Dots mark high and low tide at the nearest tide gauge.

Day and night

Switches at sunrise and sunset. Night mode is all red, to save your night vision.

Any day

Tap the center and turn the crown to see other days. Tap again for the day's times.

Private. Everything is calculated on your watch, and nothing leaves it. Privacy policy

Get it

Nightwatch is in testing before its Google Play release, and testers are welcome. You'll need a watch with Wear OS 6 or later.

  1. Join the Nightwatch testers group with the Google account on your watch.
  2. Once you're approved, open the test links for both apps and accept the invitation. Links coming soon.
  3. Install Nightwatch Companion first, then the Nightwatch watch face. Open the companion once and allow location.
